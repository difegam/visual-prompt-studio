This page is intentionally marked as a draft and is excluded from normal builds. Do not publish it as a privacy policy without replacing it with information that matches the deployed site's data practices.

Before publishing, describe the operator, the information the site collects, the purposes and legal bases where applicable, service providers, retention, user choices, contact details, and how policy changes are communicated. Remove sections that do not apply and add any required disclosures for your jurisdiction and services.

This starter has Google Tag Manager and Disqus disabled. Its sample contact form has no submission endpoint until one is configured. Recheck the policy whenever analytics, embeds, forms, hosting, or other services change.
