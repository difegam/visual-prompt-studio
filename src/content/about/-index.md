Visual Prompt Studio helps you write image prompts that have a point of view. You describe what you are making, pick a format, a style, type and colors, and the app assembles a structured prompt you can copy into ChatGPT or another image model.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server, and your recipe stays on your device until you export it.

## How it works

Generic AI images usually come from vague prompts. The Studio splits the decisions into the same seven lines every time:

1. **Format**: orientation and aspect ratio
2. **What it is**: purpose and context
3. **Theme**: composition and content
4. **Style**: a layered visual direction
5. **Exact text**: the wording, in quotation marks
6. **Typography and palette**: fonts and colors
7. **Avoid**: what must not appear

Styles are built in three layers: a design or layout language (Art Deco, Swiss, Editorial), an artistic movement (Cubism, Surrealism, Pixel Art), and a technique or finish (Screenprint, Risograph, Cyanotype). Mixing one from each layer gives you something more specific than "make it look cool".

## Where it started

The project began with two Spanish-language resources:

- The video [Deja de generar imágenes genéricas con ChatGPT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FX8c_ytLuIQ).
- The beailabs newsletter post [Crear imágenes con ChatGPT: estilos, prompts y ejemplos](https://beailabs.beehiiv.com/p/crear-imagenes-chatgpt) by Beatriz Ruiz García.

The seven-line structure and the three-layer view of style come from that material. Read [the story behind the Studio](/blog/from-generic-to-art-directed-prompts) for how they turned into an app, and go to the originals for the full explanations and examples.
